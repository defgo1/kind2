How it works

Swipe the covers to choose a story, then press Play story. The screen locks onto the cover while the story plays, so little hands can't change anything. To unlock, press and hold the lock in the top corner for 3 seconds.

Put it on the home screen

Lock the whole device (recommended)

The app ignores taps on the story screen, but no app on a web page can block the home swipe or home button. For a full lock, also turn on the device's own lock:

  1. iPhone / iPad – Guided Access: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On. Start a story, then triple-click the side (or home) button and tap Start. Triple-click again to finish.
  2. Android – App pinning: Settings → Security → App pinning → On. Open the app, open Recents, tap the app's icon and choose Pin.
